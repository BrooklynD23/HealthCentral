## Key Features

**Getting your records in**

- **Document import** — upload lab results, visit notes, imaging and pathology
  reports (PDF or image) for automatic extraction
- **Structured import** — bring in CSV lab exports and FHIR bundles from a
  patient portal, with exact values instead of OCR guesses
- **Verification** — nothing is treated as fact until you confirm it, and every
  extracted value shows the exact sentence it came from

**Making sense of them**

- **Trends** — see values over time against reference ranges
- **Timeline** — one chronological view of labs, visits, imaging and medication
  changes
- **Highlights** — the parts of a document that matter, surfaced as chips
- **Interpretations & assistant** — plain-language explanations, always cited
  back to your own results or a reference source, never diagnosis or dosing
  advice
- **Search** — find anything across your whole record

**Acting on them**

- **Medications** — track medications, schedules and adherence
- **Follow-up tasks** — turn a clinician's instructions into a checklist, in
  their own words
- **Pinboards** — group everything relevant to one concern

**Sharing and keeping**

- **Export** — clinician-ready summaries, visit-prep packets, FHIR R4, CSV/JSON
- **Recovery code** — a one-time code, shown at profile creation, that gets you
  back in if you forget your password
- **Delete everything** — you can permanently erase a profile and its records
- **Privacy first** — all data encrypted locally on your device; nothing is
  uploaded, and there is no account, no sync and no telemetry

## A note on what this app does not do

Asclexis is educational. It explains what your results say and what a
reference source says about them. It does not diagnose, does not recommend
treatment or dosing, and is not a substitute for your clinician.
